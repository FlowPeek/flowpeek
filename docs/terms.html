Updates

The app checks once a day for a new version and tells you when one exists. You choose whether to install it, and you can turn automatic checks off. Updates are delivered from the releases page above and are verified against the project's signing key before they are applied.

The optional AI feature

FlowPeek can ask a language model to write Mermaid source for you. It is off until you switch it on, and it uses an API key you supply for an account you hold with that provider. When you use it, your prompt goes to that provider and their terms and pricing apply to that request. FlowPeek is not a party to your relationship with them, and any cost you incur there is yours.
